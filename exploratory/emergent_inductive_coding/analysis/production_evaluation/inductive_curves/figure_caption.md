# Deductive Coverage and Retrospective Inductive Theme Accumulation

**Figure:** `deductive_coverage_and_inductive_accumulation.png`

**Panel A** shows deductive coverage against the fixed a-priori codebook: the number of
the 11 pre-specified subthemes observed as focus groups are added. **Panels B–F** show
`LLM_ASSISTED_RETROSPECTIVE_OPEN_THEMATIC_ACCUMULATION` under
`CANONICAL_RESOLVED_LOWER` for Q1–Q5: the number of classified open-theme clusters
observed as focus groups are added.

In every panel the human line is **one realisation** (FG1–FG5). Each synthetic condition
is **three independent study realisations** (R1, R2, R3): the line is their median and
the shaded band is their full range. Replicates are never pooled before a curve is built.
Within a realisation the value at each position is the mean over that realisation's own
orderings — 120 for Q1, Q2, Q3 and Q5, and 24 for Q4. **Q4 ends at four focus groups**
(FG1–FG4); synthetic FG5 Q4 units were extracted but stay outside the curve because no
paired human FG5 Q4 exists.

## What this figure states

- **Deductive coverage and inductive accumulation are not equivalent measures.** Panel A
  counts how much of a closed, pre-specified codebook has been seen; it is bounded at 11
  by construction. Panels B–F count clusters induced from the material itself, with no
  ceiling. The two y-axes measure different things and the panels must not be read as a
  single quantity.
- **This figure does not demonstrate saturation achieved**, for any curve. None of
  these panels is evidence that emergence had stabilised.
- **The inductive curves exclude 86 unresolved raw themes under LOWER.** They are
  reported separately and enter no scenario.
- **Shaded synthetic bands are ranges across three study realisations, not confidence
  intervals.** They describe how much three runs of the same design differed, not the
  precision of an estimate.

## Conclusion

- Fixed-codebook coverage does not assess saturation.
- Retrospective inductive accumulation continued through the final focus-group position
  in most questions and realisations.
- This study does not establish stabilisation of code emergence.
- Meaning saturation was not assessed.
